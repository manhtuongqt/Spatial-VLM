# Test-IID visual preview

These five images are visual previews of the intended Test-IID distribution.
They come from independent calibration captures with the same asset pool,
layout generator and camera bin as development data. They are **not** official
Test-IID samples and must be excluded from any future test manifest because
they have now been inspected.

| Panel | Sample | Relation/query summary |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `v211cal_family_000001__clean` | apple `front_of` purple cube |
| 2 | `v211cal_family_000006__clean` | mug `nearer_than_both` lemon and yellow cube |
| 3 | `v211cal_family_000008__clean` | blue cube `behind` green cube |
| 4 | `v211cal_family_000040__clean` | apple `right_of` purple cube |
| 5 | `v211cal_family_000100__clean` | mustard bottle `between_in_depth` yellow and green cubes |

Official Test-IID must be newly generated with disjoint family IDs, seeds,
instructions, captures and layout fingerprints.
